About this policy

This policy applies to Lepas Maghrib, Android package com.lepasmaghrib.game, version 1.0.0, published by the developer using the GitHub account marzuqikram.

Data collected and shared

The release game operates offline. It does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting services. It does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. The Android release does not request Internet permission.

The game stores settings such as language, graphics and presentation preferences, and may store game progress locally in its app storage. These files support gameplay and are not sent to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and saved progress remain until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. You can delete them through Android Settings → Apps → Lepas Maghrib → Storage → Clear storage. This resets your settings and progress. The developer holds no remotely collected gameplay data to delete.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains supernatural horror and frightening encounters; consult its Google Play content rating before playing.
